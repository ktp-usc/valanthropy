About VAlanthropy

VAlanthropy is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization dedicated to developing affordable veteran housing communities that promote healing, stability, independence, and long-term success.

Our work focuses on creating permanent housing solutions for veterans who need more than temporary assistance. We believe stable housing provides the foundation for better health, employment, stronger community connections, and lasting recovery.

Our Mission

VAlanthropy develops affordable veteran housing communities that create pathways to stability, independence, and long-term success.

Our Vision

To create a replicable veteran housing model that strengthens communities and expands opportunities for veterans beyond our first project.

Hidden Wounds Village Alpha

Hidden Wounds Village Alpha is VAlanthropy’s flagship housing initiative located in Sumter, South Carolina.

The project provides permanent affordable housing within a supportive community designed to help veterans achieve long-term stability.

VAlanthropy leads the development, infrastructure planning, construction, furnishing, funding strategy, and long-term sustainability of the project.

Housing Development With Accountability

As the lead development organization behind Hidden Wounds Village Alpha, VAlanthropy oversees every stage of the project, including:

  • Housing Development
  • Infrastructure Planning
  • Construction Coordination
  • Apartment Furnishing
  • Project Funding Strategy
  • Sponsor and Donor Support
  • Long-term Sustainability Planning
  • Community Housing Model Development

Our service partners provide veteran support through intake, care coordination, referrals, and ongoing resident services while VAlanthropy remains focused on housing development and project leadership.
